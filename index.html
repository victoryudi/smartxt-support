Help with Smartxt

A few useful answers.

Can I record before setting up AI?

Yes. Recording and local storage do not require an AI API key. Add a supported key in Settings when you want to transcribe or generate.

Which services does Smartxt use?

OpenAI handles audio transcription and optional speech. Text generation uses your configured OpenAI or Anthropic account. You can optionally connect Notion. If iCloud is available, your library syncs through your private iCloud account.

What happens to completed audio?

Smartxt removes app-managed audio after the transcript and report have been safely saved and matched to the processed recording. The extracted text, tags and report remain. Failed or unfinished processing keeps the recording for retry. Files you exported or originals outside the app are separate copies.

How do I report a problem?

Email labasedrop@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, Smartxt version, and the steps that caused the problem. Never include API keys. Only attach recordings or transcripts if you deliberately want support to receive that content.
