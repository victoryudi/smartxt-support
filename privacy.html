Privacy policy

Your recordings and text belong to you.

This policy describes Smartxt’s iPhone, iPad and Mac app. Smartxt is supported by LA BASE LLC. The current Apple developer distribution account is held by Victor Yudi Tomo.

What stays on your device

Smartxt saves recordings, imported media, transcripts, generated reports, tags, notes, processing progress, and settings in the app’s local storage. Microphone access is used only for recording you initiate. API keys and integration credentials are stored in Apple Keychain, not in report or settings files. Supported credentials may sync to your devices through iCloud Keychain when available.

When content leaves your device

When you choose a transcription or generation action, Smartxt sends the content needed for that action directly to your configured provider using your API credentials. Audio is sent to OpenAI for transcription. Text, relevant context, and instructions are sent to OpenAI or Anthropic for generation. Speech playback may send text to OpenAI. If enabled, fallback generation can send that request to your selected fallback provider.

Your provider can associate these requests with your provider account. Provider retention, security, billing and privacy terms apply. Smartxt does not provide anonymous processing or promise that providers delete all request data immediately.

iCloud and optional integrations

When iCloud is available for Smartxt, library content and settings can sync automatically to your private iCloud database, including unprocessed audio. Apple operates this service under your iCloud account. You can manage iCloud access in your device’s Apple Account settings.

When you connect Notion and select a destination, Smartxt reads authorized context and sends reports to that destination when you request saving or enable automatic saving. Notion retains those pages until you manage or delete them there.

Development installations may offer a separately enabled private Home Hub backup integration. That integration is unavailable in public release builds. Existing development backups remain subject to their own storage and deletion controls.

Retention and deletion

Unprocessed recordings stay saved so you can resume later. After Smartxt durably saves the completed transcript and report and verifies they match the source, it removes the associated app-managed audio and eligible matching revisions. Transcripts, reports and tags remain available. Incomplete, failed or unverified processing preserves source media for recovery.

Recordings you delete first move to Recently Deleted. Permanently erasing one removes its local recording data and queues deletion for iCloud sync when available. Offline devices and pending sync can delay removal of remote copies. Prior external exports, Notion pages, provider retention, system backups, and private development backup deliveries are separate and are not erased by deleting the local item. Manage those copies in the relevant service.

You can remove stored API keys and clear local history in Settings. If you uninstall the app, local app-container data is removed by the operating system; Keychain and iCloud data may remain and must be managed separately.

Tracking and diagnostics

Smartxt includes no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking SDK. We do not sell your recordings or use them for advertising. Apple may provide TestFlight feedback, crashes and diagnostics under its own settings and terms. Providers may maintain operational and security logs under their policies.

Support and your choices

If you contact us, we receive the contact information and content you voluntarily send and use it to respond to your request. Do not send API keys. Request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us. Avoid submitting recordings containing sensitive information or another person’s content unless you have permission to share it.

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children. You may use local recording without configuring AI providers. Third-party features are governed by the account and age requirements of their respective services.
